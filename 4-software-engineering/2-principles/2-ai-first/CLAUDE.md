# AI-first

Giving AI light but opinionated direction is a great way to keep control of it while leveraging what it can do. That direction is what I call principles; this node is a list of them.

## Why principles work now

Aligning humans with each other on principles has always been hard. AI is more willing to follow a human's direction, so principles do two jobs:

- they keep a clear direction;
- they show where that direction hurts from its own limits, which is when to introduce the complexity it lacks. Not before.

## Engineers maintain AI systems

An engineer now maintains the AI system that builds the software. The work is to iterate on the rules given to agents, and to block them as little as possible.

For example, automated review, with its feedback addressed automatically, is the new norm. A human can still read the review, but to make the automated review better, not to do its job by hand.

# iam

Where the client meets [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/domains/iam/CLAUDE|IAM]]. Like every server function, these compose the domain and run through `run`; the middleware has already put the request's Session on the context.

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `iam.ts` | `signUp`, `signIn`, `signOut`, which the composer's `/logout` runs, and `session`, the server functions, and `issueKey`, `keys` and `revokeKey`, the Account's Keys. Their schemas bound the strings; what an email, a password or a Key's name must be is IAM's to say, on the field. A Key's id is letters and digits, as Better Auth makes them |
| `organizations/` | Organizations (HEX-136). `organizations.ts`, the server functions and their schemas: `createOrganization`, its name bounded as a Title is, answering its star; `standingIn`, where the Account stands in the Organization a star names, IAM's `standingIn`, its role, or `outside` it, none for any other star, which offers the star's Members and Groups widgets, or Ask to join (HEX-138); `members`, each by their star's card, their role and whether it is the reader, for its members; `addMember`, `setMemberRole` and `removeMember`, each naming the Organization and the member by their stars, never an id of IAM's; `askToJoin`, the Organization by its star, one of its Groups by its id, and `decideJoinRequest`, a request by its id, a UUID IAM made, which an admin's timeline names, accepted or declined (HEX-138). `programs.ts`, the programs: each starts from IAM's `sessionOnly`, finds the Organization a star names (Mapping's `ownerOf`, then IAM's `isOrganization`, `OrganizationNotFound` otherwise, a member and a stranger alike) and the Account a member's star names, then IAM decides, in one transaction; a creation makes the Organization in IAM and its star in Mapping (`starNamed`) in one. `organizations.test.ts`, through `run` over PGlite, Accounts signed up there: made with its star, its admin owning its System and sharing it, its writes told in no Conversation of its own, a stranger shown its card alone, a member reading and writing what a Grant to its Members, named by its star, lets them, an admin demoted or a member removed holding from the next request, the members listed by their stars, the last admin kept, a Key refused. And its Groups of its own (HEX-137): `groups.ts`, their server functions and schemas, `groups`, each with who is in it by their stars, and whether the reader asked to join it, for its members; `createGroup`, answering its id, `renameGroup`, `deleteGroup` and `setInGroup`, a member named by their star, a Group by its id (`GroupId`, IAM's), a name bounded in UTF-16 units before IAM counts it; `groupsToShareWith`, the Groups the Account may name in a Grant, by each Organization's star's card; their programs in `programs.ts`, each through `organizationAt` and one transaction. `groups.test.ts`, through `run` over PGlite: the grill's scenario with a real Group, a Grant to it reaching whoever is in it on the canvas and through a Key, as the MCP reads, and nobody else; who is in it, and its deletion, holding from the next request, the who-reaches list then saying a Grant set to it gone, its owner clearing it and naming it again refused; a Group named by its Organization's members alone; changed by its admins alone; a Key refused. `joining.test.ts`, joining and leaving (HEX-138), through `run` over PGlite, the bus's work waited for: a request told to every admin, how it stands read anew with each one's day, decided by one alone, from a Session, the other's day saying so, whoever asked told the outcome; accepted, membership and what the Organization's Grants let it read; declined, nothing; a second open one refused; a Group's asked by a member; someone added or taken out told, leaving told nothing; a removed member's read and their running Turn's next write refused; the last admin who joined kept, someone who joined by asking taking that place |
| `iam.test.ts` | The programs through `run`, on the runtime's Better Auth over PGlite, one browser per device: signed out, sign-up and the Session its cookie proves, sign-in and sign-out on another device, every refusal as it crosses the wire; a Key issued, listed and revoked, `SessionRequired` for a request a Key proves; the schemas' bounds |

The client's side, home's read of the Session, which shows a signed-out visit the screen signed out, and the way back once signed in, is `front/client/iam/guard.ts`: [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]].

## Rules

- **A guarded page's server functions guard themselves.** Each starts with IAM's `signedIn`, or `sessionOnly` for the Session and the Keys; the page's `beforeLoad` is for the user, not for security.
- **A Key's secret crosses the wire once**, in `issueKey`'s answer. `keys` never carries it.

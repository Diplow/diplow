# assistant

Someone builds their System by talking to an agent instead of clicking every Tile into place. Before the agent can help, it needs to know what happened: what the user said, what changed in the System and who changed it, where the user went on the canvas. Assistant keeps that record, the **Conversation**, and it is the agent's only memory: a Turn starts afresh and reads it.

The language, as [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/STACK|STACK]] first told it:

- **Conversation**: one continuous timeline per Account, added with its first Entry, read a **day** at a time, a day spanning from its reader's midnight to the next: a date of their calendar and how far their clock stands from UTC at its midnight and at the next, so the day their clock changes spans 23 or 25 hours and every instant falls on one day (`Day`, `dayAt`, `spanOf`). Earlier days stay as they were written.
- **Entry**: one moment of the timeline, oldest first, each dating from an instant. A **Message**, by the user or by the agent, trimmed, never empty, at most 10,000 characters, which may link Tiles. A **change** to a System, whoever made it: its verb, as Mapping names the event (`TileMoved`), the Tile it is about by its id and the Title it had then, the other Tile a swap traded places with or a Reference points at, and who acted; made by the user in someone else's System, it says so, and made by someone else in the user's, it offers its Undo, once (`undone`). An **import**: the Tile it landed as, and how many Tiles came with it. A **navigation**: where the user went, consecutive gestures merged into one Entry. A **Turn's end**, when not by its answer: a Stop, its ceiling, the month's allowance, its time or its sandbox (`TurnEnded`, `BadEnd`). A **Proposal**: its changes, its fate and, once accepted, whether the user undid it (below). What a **Turn applied**, told once it ended: the Turn, how many changes its writes made, and whether the user undid them (`TurnApplied`). A **Tile shared** with the user, told once, when a Grant first opened it to them: who shared it, by the Title of their star then, the Tile, by its id and the Title it had, which stands in their System, not the user's, and the rights the user has there, as the API layer hands them over from Mapping's (`Shared`, HEX-131); changing it later, or taking it back, says nothing.
- **Link**: a Tile a Message points at, the user having `@`ed it, kept inside its text by the Tile's id alone, `@[<id>]` (`linkTo`), since a Tile's Title and folder change whenever it is renamed or moved; its first fifty are read, the rest as written. Whoever reads the Message joins in the Title the System holds then, none for a Tile deleted since, which the timeline shows broken. A Message **opening** with a link moves the Turn answering it into that Tile (`leadingLink`, `turnLocation`); any other link points its agent at the Tile, whose current path its prompt gives.
- **Who acted**, as the timeline shows it: "you" for a write a Session made, a Key by its name for one a Key made, read when the write is recorded, and absent once that Key was revoked, and "Assistant" for one a Turn's Key made, or an accepted Proposal's (`OwnActor`); and **someone else**, another Account writing in the user's System, which a Grant let them write, by the Title their star had then and who of theirs acted, themselves, one of their Keys or their agent (`Someone`, HEX-132). A write the user made in someone else's System is theirs, "you", the Entry saying it was made elsewhere (`elsewhere`), its Tiles standing there.
- **Turn**: one run of the agent answering a Message, started afresh in the Tile it is given, its **location**: the Tile the Message opens with a link to, else the one centered on the view it was sent from, the Conversation its only memory. It records when it started, what it spent on the model, in millionths of a dollar, what its agent is doing now, its **step** (`TurnStep`: the tool it runs, a read or a shell command, and on what), shown live and dropped as the agent speaks again, and how it ended (`TurnEnd`): its answer, a Stop, its own ceiling, the month's allowance, its time, or the sandbox failing, the timeline telling each but the answer in an Entry of its own; nothing is rolled back. It runs in its **Mode**, the one its Conversation was in as it started (below). One runs at most per Conversation: starting another meanwhile is `TurnRunning`, and none starts once the month's allowance is spent, `AllowanceSpent`. It runs `settings.turn.longest` at most, 15 minutes, its **deadline**, which its Key, IAM's Turn's Key, proves it until; one still running past it is over (`liveTurn`), and ends by its time once another starts, its Entry dated from its deadline.
- **Agent**: what runs a Turn, Claude Code, started detached in the Account's sandbox, in the location's folder, by a small launcher that posts each line it writes back to the app as it comes, so the answer is saved with the tab closed ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/domains/assistant/agent/CLAUDE|agent]]). It is told the rules of the Turn, its Mode among them, where it starts, the Tiles the Message links and where each lives now, the Tiles on the user's screen and today's latest Entries (`settings.turn.entries`, 50), on the settings' model, Sonnet 5.5; its text becomes its Messages, its result ends the Turn. A **Stop** interrupts it, so it ends cleanly, then kills it past a grace.
- **Spend**: what a Turn's model calls cost, in millionths of a dollar, each call's usage priced by the settings' table once its answer has ended and added to its Turn. A Turn may spend until its **ceiling**, `settings.turn.ceiling`, $1; an Account's Turns until the month's **allowance**, `settings.month.allowance`, $10 a calendar month, UTC, a setting until credits exist. The month's spend is the sum of its Turns', never a counter of its own. A call **reserves** the most it may cost before it goes, its body's length as input at the dearest input price and its `max_tokens` as output, and is charged what it used once its answer has ended, or the output it may have answered when the answer was cut short, what it reserved then released. A Turn whose spend is at a limit is held back and ends so, its ceiling named first; a call that would pass a limit with what the calls under way reserved **waits** for them, the Turn going on; a call with none under way never waits, so it may end past a limit, by what it used beyond its spend.
- **Model call**: what a Turn's agent asks the model, through the app's proxy: its body let through only as the settings name it, its fields, its models, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 4.5, its client tools alone and no server tool, its betas, `max_tokens` capped (`spending/call.ts`).
- **Mode**, per Conversation, as in Claude Code: *ask*, the default (`firstMode`), or *apply* (`Mode`). A Turn takes the Mode its Conversation is in as it starts, and keeps it until it ends; switching it changes the next Turn. Whether a write a Turn's Key made is drafted or applied is Assistant's decision, its **route** (`routeOf`, `route`): drafted by a Turn in ask, applied by one in apply; the API layer routes on it, and no MCP tool asks the Mode.
- **Proposal**: the writes a Turn's agent made in ask, kept as a draft until the user decides it: at most one open per Conversation, opened by the first draft, with an Entry of its own, and continued by every later one, a later Turn's included, so the next Turn reads through it and adds to it. Each write is kept as the API layer hands it, opaque: Mapping's Operation, which Assistant never reads, and the changes it makes, as Mapping summarized them on the System the Proposal makes (`ProposedChange`). The user accepts it whole or discards it; its **fate** (`Fate`) is open, accepted or discarded, its Entry saying once the user undid an accepted one, and a decided one is `ProposalClosed` to a second decision. Switching to apply leaves it open. Its Entry follows it, its changes as they are drafted, its fate once decided and, accepted, whether the user undid it; with a Turn's `TurnApplied`, which says it once undone, the Entries that change after they are written.
- **Undo**: what the Assistant applied, an applied Turn's writes or an accepted Proposal's, and a write someone else made in the user's System, undone whole, once, as the user's own change. Someone else's write is kept with what undoes it by its Entry's id, as it is recorded, in the write's own transaction (`undo/`'s `writtenBySomeone`), and its Entry offers the Undo at once, never `/undo`, which undoes the agent's last change. Assistant keeps what undoes it as the API layer hands it, opaque, Mapping's inverse, with the Turn or the Proposal (`applied`); the Entry that offers it, the accepted Proposal's at once, a `TurnApplied` one once its Turn ended having changed anything (`offeredNow`), says so once undone (`undoneEntry`), and offers no second Undo: `NothingToUndo`, as for a Turn still running or one that applied nothing (`undoing`). A Tile it touched changed since refuses it, Mapping's to say (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-16`).
- **Merged navigation**: the canvas names each gesture and the Tile it was made on; the browser merges them as the user goes, by the **merge rule** (`merged`): every gesture counted, the latest twelve kept, in order. It sends the merged Entry when something else enters the timeline, a Message or a write, or when the page is hidden, never one request per gesture. A navigation keeps a Tile by its id alone; whoever reads a day joins in the Titles the System holds then.

Assistant knows no Tile but by its id, opaque (`TileRef`): it never reads one, nor checks an id against the System, which is Mapping's to do where it matters, as the API layer does for a Turn's location. What it holds of a Tile beyond its id, a Title, a Preview, a file, it declares in its own words, `Summarized`, `Glimpse`, `Linked`, and the API layer hands it Mapping's values, which satisfy them (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-18`). A change and an import are recorded as Mapping summarized them when they happened, never rebuilt from the System, so the timeline is a record of the past: a Tile deleted since keeps the Title it had. The API layer, which alone composes domains, hands Assistant that summary ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/api/assistant/CLAUDE|assistant]]). A gesture is a word of the canvas's (`center`, `show-context`) that Assistant keeps as it came, without knowing it.

The Mode is a column of `conversation`, and the Proposals a fourth table, `proposal`, through the Proposals repository, whose index lets one be open at most per Conversation; a change to either locks the Conversation's row first, so two drafts never decide on one Proposal at once (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-15`). What the Assistant applied lives in a fifth, `applied`, through the Applied repository, one row per applied Turn or accepted Proposal, locked while Assistant decides whether to undo it (`#DEC-16`). It keeps an Entry's content as Assistant encodes it, and Assistant decodes it by the same schema; one it cannot read back is a defect. Why the content is one JSON column is `hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-8`.

## Rules

- **The Conversation is the user's.** It is read and written for the Account the request's Session proves; a Key, which proves a program, reads and writes none of it. A change made with a Key still lands in the Conversation of the Account it acted for, labeled by the Key's name. Two Entries come from another Account's request: a Tile shared, which the API layer records in the Conversation of the Account it was shared with, once the Grant committed, that Account reading it as its own and the one who shared it nothing of it; and a write someone else made in the Account's System, recorded in the owner's Conversation in the write's own transaction, the Conversation locked before the System, as an accept and an undo take the two, with what undoes it (HEX-132).
- **An Entry dates from when it happened.** A Message and a change date from when they were recorded; a merged navigation from its last gesture, which only the browser's clock saw, so the browser sends how long ago that was, never when, and the server's clock dates it: two clocks never disagree on the order.
- **A Turn is the user's.** It starts from the Account the request's Session proves, an Account entitled to the Assistant, which the API layer checks with IAM before Assistant hears of it; it ends wherever it runs, for the Account and the Turn its Key proves, needing no Session: its agent's lines reach Assistant through the events door, its Key their only proof.
- **A Turn's secret stays in its process.** The Turn's Key enters the sandbox in the launcher's environment alone, never in its prompt nor a file (`agent/launch.ts`); no other secret enters it.
- **The Mode and the Proposal are decided by the Decider.** Every rule of them lives in `operations/decider.ts`: a draft continues the open Proposal and opens one only when none is, and only the open one is accepted or discarded, once; the service runs it on the Conversation it locked. Recording an Entry refuses nothing a schema has not already bounded, and a Turn refuses only to run beside another, a rule the `turn` table's index holds, or once the month's allowance is spent (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-11`).
- **What was applied is an aggregate of its own, outside the Decider.** One `applied` row per applied Turn, accepted Proposal or write of someone else's, the last keyed by its Entry's id, locked while it is decided, its rules the pure functions of `entities/applied/undo.ts` (`offeredNow`, `offeringEntry`, `undoing`, `undoneEntry`), run by `undo/` and the service, as a Turn's rules live outside the Decider too; the Decider decides the Mode and the Proposal alone (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-23`).
- **An ended Turn writes nothing.** A write of a Turn, drafted or applied, reads the Turn once the Conversation's lock is held and is refused `TurnOver` once it ended (`stillRunning`), whoever called: the API layer passes the refusal on, and no caller checks a boolean (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-22`).
- **A Proposal's writes are opaque.** Assistant keeps each as the API layer hands it and gives it back in order, never reading it: what it does to the System, folded over it, drafted or run, is Mapping's, and the API layer composes the two (`api/assistant/proposal/`). A model call is decided by pure functions of `spending/`, and answered as an `Admission` rather than an error: the proxy words every answer as Anthropic would, and no client of the app ever reads one (`hexframe-app-assistant/decisions.md#DEC-12`).
- **A Turn spends what it used.** A call is settled once its answer has ended, a Stop's cancel included, and after the Turn ended too: what a call cost is spent whether or not the Turn still runs. A call the relay failed to make releases what it reserved at once; should the settling fail, the Turn keeps the reservation.

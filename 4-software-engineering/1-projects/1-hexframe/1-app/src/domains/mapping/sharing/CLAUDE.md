# sharing

How a Tile's Grants change, a concept of [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/domains/mapping/CLAUDE|Mapping]] the API layer calls as directly as the service, whose folder is full, as it calls `landing/`; and what an actor may read, which the service itself reads by (`reading.ts`). What a Grant is and how it resolves is `entities/rights/`'s; who may change one, `operations/rights/may.ts`'s `mayShare`: the System's owner, from a Session, never through a Key, a Turn's Key or an agent.

## Rules

- **No Operation.** Setting a Grant is neither proposed nor undone, makes no event of a System's, and leaves the System's Version where it was: the owner's own read of the System shows nothing of it, and a visitor reads the System again once the Version they are shown of it moves, which a Grant changing what they see moves (`sightVersion`, HEX-132). The one thing it publishes is `TileShared`, a fact of sharing rather than of a System, when a Grant first opens a Tile to an Account, so their timeline tells them once; the owner's timeline tells nothing (`hexframe-app-sharing-organizations/decisions.md#DEC-8`).
- **Someone is named by their star.** A change names an Account by its star's id, its Handle, never by an id of IAM's: Mapping finds the owner the star names, so a Tile below a star never stands for anyone, and a star's card, which anyone sees, is all a wrong guess tells.
- **Under the System's lock.** A Grant is written with the System's star locked, as a change is, so a move deciding who it opens a Tile to (`allowed`) reads the Grants as they stand.
- **Existing Systems stay private.** No Grant exists until the owner sets one; nothing here sets one of its own.
- **A star is found by its card, nothing more.** A search reads the Roots alone and selects their id, Title and Preview, so no Body and no Tile below a star leaves the database; a link leads to a star only through `shownTile`, so a Tile kept from the reader says nothing, not even which System holds it.

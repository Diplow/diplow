# layouts

The layouts no single side owns, each a shell with slots that decides where things go and how they fold on a phone: those the Chat and the Canvas share, and those whose only users are pages the one screen retires, which stay where they fit until their last user goes (`DataTable` and `PageHeader` went with the Keys and sign-in pages, HEX-103). A layout one side alone uses lives in that side's `layouts/` ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/CLAUDE|ui]], "Rules") and comes here once the other side uses it too. A layout imports atoms, the seams, the files at `ui/`'s root and, in its `gallery.tsx`, the page's frame in `gallery/`, never a side (dependency-cruiser, `no-layouts-importing-up`).

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `screen/` | `Screen.tsx`, `Screen`, hexframe's one screen, a shell with four slots, `canvas`, `corner`, `messages` and `composer`: wide, the chat a column on the left cut along one curve, the canvas filling the screen behind it right of the curve, the corner top right; below `sm` of the screen's own width (a container query, so a specimen folds as a phone does), one side in front, which the caller holds (`front`, `onFrontChange`), the chat with the canvas showing through a strip at its top, or the canvas with the composer kept at its foot, a seam between them that a tap, a drag or the keyboard turns, the side behind `inert`, and the canvas ending above the composer, whose height the screen measures (`--screen-composer`), and above the seam (`--screen-seam`), whichever side is in front; the conversation opens at its foot and stays there as it grows, lines read after the first paint included, or as the foot below it grows, until the reader scrolls up or acts in a conversation too short to scroll, and writing at the foot brings it back. What a slot shares with the screen it reads from it: `--screen-clear`, how far the chat's foot keeps its content in from the curve, which the foot hands to what sits there as `--screen-foot-inset`, wide only, so the Composer and the sign-in widget add it to their padding and no caller pads them (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-36`), and `cornerPlace`, where the canvas's corner sits, which Help and the specimens take too. `state/useScreenState.ts`, `useScreenState`, the side in front, which a caller holds the screen's `front` with: the chat when the screen opens, turned by the seam (`turn`), the canvas brought forward when the chat points at it (`cross`, with what it showed, for a screen reader), then the chat given back after a glance (`--duration-glance`), unless the canvas was taken meanwhile, by the seam or a hand, a crossing meanwhile looking again from the start and a canvas already in front staying as it was (HEX-108), home's and the specimens'; and `state/latest.ts`, `keptAtFoot`, pure, whether the conversation is kept at its foot after what the reader did; each tested beside it. `curve.ts`, the curve, pure and tested: its offset from the chat's edge and how far it reaches, the cut as a `clip-path` in percentages, its SVG path and the track beside the messages, and the star's place and the scroll it gives. `Scrollbar.tsx`, `Scrollbar`, the hairline drawn along it and the star riding the stretch beside the messages, lighting what shows, held and dragged, a click on the track scrolling there; hidden from the accessibility tree, since the messages stay a focusable scroller. The width it turns wide at is written once, `--container-wide` (`@wide:`, `@max-wide:`), and the seam's display tells the code. A crossing's glance is the caller's too (HEX-108): the screen says what it showed in a live region (`said`), a hand on the canvas meanwhile, a pointer or a key, takes it (`onFrontChange`), and the focus the chat held when it went inert comes back with it, unless the focus went elsewhere meanwhile. Its specimens sit in `/design-system`'s route, which may lay out both sides |
| `HoverCard.tsx` | `HoverCard`, what a tile cuts short, in full, beside it, on Radix: its place, title, preview and its notes, each in the colour of what it says (a clash, a Reference, the agent at work; `CardNote`); after 420ms under the pointer, at once on keyboard focus, never on a click or under a finger; for the eye alone, the pointer going through it, while what it tells of describes itself to a screen reader with the same words (`aria-describedby`, in a `<desc>` inside an SVG); handing on what a trigger around it hands it, shut while that trigger's menu is open, and telling its caller it opened or shut (`onOpenChange`). Beside a tile on the canvas, on its right; above a tile named in the chat (`side`), so the next words stay within reach. The canvas's since HEX-96, here since a tile named in the chat opened it too (HEX-108) |
| `Dialog.tsx` | `Dialog`, the one thing that stops the user, on Radix's AlertDialog, controlled: a kicker, the act asked as a question, a paragraph of consequence, what it touches drawn and counted, a name to type for what is hardest to undo, then the quiet way out and the act (`Act`). Wide, over the conversation's column from its left edge, so the canvas stays in view; below `sm`, a sheet from the bottom. Focus starts on the way out, or on the name to type; Enter confirms from the button only; a click on the dim leaves until a name is typed |
| `ConfirmDialog.tsx` | `ConfirmDialog`, a question asked before something is done: its trigger, a title, a description and the confirming button, destructive when it deletes. `Dialog` supersedes it once its users, a Proposal's discard, a Tile's delete and a Key's revoke, move onto it |
| `states.tsx` | What a place shows instead of its content: `EmptyState`, nothing yet and how to add the first; `ErrorState`, a read that failed, with a retry and the request id; `Forbidden`, a read the user may not make |
| `gallery.tsx` | `LayoutsGallery`, every layout here in its states, on `/design-system`: the hover card on a tile, and above a line naming two tiles |

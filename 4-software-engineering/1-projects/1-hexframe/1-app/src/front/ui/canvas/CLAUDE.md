# canvas

The Canvas, the side of hexframe's screen a System is seen through ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/CLAUDE|ui]], "hexframe's screen, in words"). It never imports the Chat (dependency-cruiser, `no-side-importing-the-other`): a piece both use moves to `ui/atoms/` or `ui/layouts/`. HEX-9 picked SVG: one `<svg>` whose viewBox is the canvas's own coordinates, every hex a `<polygon>`, and the text as HTML inside a `<foreignObject>`, so it wraps like the rest of the page. The page sizes the `<svg>` with CSS and everything inside scales with it; a tile's words are fitted to the size it shows at (`useScale`). Since HEX-106 every hex is the proposed tile's (`tile/`, HEX-104), drawn by its light.

The canvas is a medium of the [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/2-claude-mod/hooks/shape/CLAUDE|shape]], as claude-mod and the Obsidian plugin are: the shape's `layoutView` (`2-claude-mod/hooks/shape/layout.ts`) places its hexes, so a System lays out in the app as a vault lays out in Claude Code and in Obsidian. `geometry/shape.ts` is the one module of the app that imports it, by relative path (dependency-cruiser): it turns the System and the view into the shape's Frames, a Tile's id standing in for a folder's path, and answers in the canvas's own words. The shape's `Direction`, `Tile`, `Frame` and `Placement` go no further than it.

## Rules

- **The view belongs to the URL, not to the canvas.** `Canvas` holds no view state: a gesture calls `onViewChange` with the next view and what asked for it, and the route navigates to it. `readCanvasView` is the route's `validateSearch`. A link from before the shape's view, its `expanded` a list of ids, keeps its center alone.
- **The view is the shape's, at depth 2** ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/STACK#A vault as a hexframe|STACK]]): the center, the Frame kind of the ring around it (`frame`: Children, or Branches or Leaves past six of them in all, as the shape offers them; none by default, the center folded), the ring inside its hex (`inner`: Context, or Leaves inside Branches, never the same kind in both; none by default), and the Branches of the ring around it that open, by Direction, each into a kind it offers (`expanded`). Nothing opens further. A folded center, and a centered Leaf, which has no ring, stand alone, large, their preview read whole, the ground left around them.
- **What is Mapping and what is not.** Tiles, Branches, Leaves, Directions and Context are Mapping's words (STACK.md); centering, the rings shown and opening a Branch are view state, and live here.
- **Gestures.** A click, Enter or Space on the center opens it into its first ring, its six blooming out of its heart, and, past six, its Leaves inside it, or folds it back alone, the ring folding into it first; on a Branch of the ring around the center, opens it into its first ring, or closes it once open, its six folding back first, but under a finger, whose tap goes in, centering it, opening in place waiting in its menu ("Open it here"); on anything else, a Leaf, a Context Tile, a member of an opened Branch, centers it, since it has nothing to open at depth 2. A centered Leaf takes no click. A double-click or Shift+Enter centers any Tile (the tile's `onGoIn`), the double-click's second click doing nothing more. A Tile a gesture centers, here, from the breadcrumb or from the chat, lands opened into its first ring, its Leaves inside past six, so its neighbours show, a Leaf alone (`centerOn`), and the camera flies there: into a child it zooms in, to a parent out; only a plain URL shows the center folded. No row picks a ring: a menu's lines that go somewhere are gestures too (`go`): Open does what a pointer's click does, Go in centers, Show its Context centers the Tile with its Context inside, or shows the center's, opening it were it folded, Back out centers the Tile the center stands under. `useCanvasState` decides each, from the hex and the view; nothing is offered a ring it doesn't have.
- **Every gesture is named.** `onViewChange(view, action)`: `action` is the `Gesture` that changed the view and the Tile it was made on: `center`, `expand` and `collapse`, the center's as a Branch's, `show-context`; `hide-context`, `show-leaves`, `hide-leaves` and `show-<kind>-around`, which no gesture makes any more, stay for the navigations recorded before. These are the words the navigation fact carries (`features/facts.ts`) and the Conversation's timeline tells; a caller that only navigates ignores them.
- **An empty slot is the caller's to use.** The canvas knows the Tile at the heart of its ring, the ring's kind and its Direction (`EmptySlotTarget`); what a click there does (add a Tile, move one there) and its accessible name come from `emptySlots`, slot by slot, as a view change comes from `onViewChange`. Without it, or where it answers nothing, empty slots stay inert, as on `/design-system`.
- **A Tile's click stays the view's.** A swap the caller offers on a Tile (`move.moving.swapTargets`) gets a button of its own, the `SwapDisc` at the foot of the hex, where no Branch shows its frame meanwhile, and a Tile stepped back while a tile moves keeps its click, so opening and centering still work while a move is under way. What a swap does is the caller's, as for an empty slot.
- **What a menu's line does is the caller's**, as for an empty slot: `acting` says what the menu says of each Tile and free slot, its frames and the lines left out, and hands the caller every chosen line but those that go somewhere. Which Tile moves is the caller's too, the URL's on home: a drag asks for it to start (`move.start`), Escape and Stop for it to end; the canvas holds what lasts the gesture, the drag, the place lit under it, the glide.
- **What is on its way is the caller's to say.** The canvas knows no write: `writes` says which Tiles a write on its way changes, which were just refused and which free slots an import lands in, and the tile draws them, a sweep after 400ms, a flare.
- **A Tile keeps its key** when its Branch opens and it is drawn at the heart of its own Frame, so keyboard focus stays on it. A Context Tile, which may be a Reference to a Tile drawn elsewhere, is keyed by its slot.
- **Colour is a token**, as everywhere in `ui/`: fills and strokes are `var(--token)` or a `color-mix` of two.
- **A wait on a motion follows its token.** What waits for a motion to end, a fold, a glide, a flare, reads that motion's duration from the stylesheet (`durationOf`, `movingFor`, in `ui/motion.ts`), never a copy of it.
- **Who touches a tile is the caller's to say**, as what is on its way: `marks` says who points at a Tile, reads, works or changed it, and what a Proposal would do; the canvas only draws it, and finds the way to a Tile it doesn't draw.

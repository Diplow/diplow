# tile

The tile and acting on it ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/canvas/CLAUDE|canvas]]): the canvas draws every hex on `Tile`, `Slot` and `Ground` since HEX-106, and, since HEX-107, each tile in its `TileMenu` and its hover card and the whole move, `Lifted`, `LandingSlot`, `SwapDisc`, `Aside` and `MoveBanner`, in place of the centered Tile's buttons and the Move mode's banner (`../hexes.tsx`). Who touches a tile, its marks, came with the crossings (HEX-108), drawn wherever its caller says (`marks` on the `Canvas`). The choices behind them are in the register, `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-27`, `#DEC-45`, `#DEC-46`, `#DEC-47` and `#DEC-48`.

## Rules

- **What Mapping decides comes in from the caller.** Whether a tile takes a new tile (`Subject.holds`), turns into the other kind (`Subject.turns`), trades places (`Seat.swaps`) or may land in a free slot (`Frame.lands`) is Mapping's `decide`, the feature's `swapsWith` and `slotOf`; the pieces here draw the answer, and the page reads it off its fixture once.
- **A finger never drags a tile, and its tap goes in.** `useDrag` ignores a touch; a finger moves a tile through its menu's frame, or "Somewhere else…" and a tap on a slot. Under `pointer-coarse` a tap goes into a tile (`clickOf`), since a finger has no double click.
- **A line's key is the tile's too.** The menu shows each line's key, and that key runs it on the focused tile, the menu shut, once per press; ⇧↵ goes in, so a tile's own Enter is the plain key alone (`buttonKeys`).
- **A drop is no click.** The click a drag let go fires on the tile it carried is taken before the tile hears it (`useDrag`).
- **What lights a tile lies over its fill, and faint under its words.** On paper a tile's fill is nearly white, opaque once lit, and hides a light behind it; so a mark's light lies over the fill, as a refusal's flare does. A light that holds, the chat pointing at a tile, stays faint where the words are, so they keep 4.5:1 in both themes, and glows from the rim in; a glint passes over them once.

# chat

The Chat, the side of hexframe's screen that shows the Conversation, where the user and the agent act ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/CLAUDE|ui]], "hexframe's screen, in words"). Its components come as the proposals for the chat land (HEX-98, HEX-99, HEX-100), and HEX-101 wires them in; until then the Conversation is drawn by `features/conversation/` from the shared pieces. The Chat never imports the Canvas (dependency-cruiser, `no-side-importing-the-other`): a piece both use moves to `ui/atoms/` or `ui/layouts/`.

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `composer/` | The Composer, proposed (HEX-99; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-10`). `Composer.tsx`, `Composer`, controlled: the field, a textarea in the user's serif, the command it opens with and each Tile it points at drawn marked under it; the field a combobox keeping the focus and the keys, the arrows moving the row, Enter or Tab taking it, Escape leaving, Enter sending what is ready, never a Stop, Shift+Enter a new line; the send hex, lit in amber once there is something to send, breathing while it is on its way, Stop while a Turn runs, the field waiting; under it the Mode, the choices its caller builds from Assistant's (ask first, apply with undo), the one on in violet, or the line saying the Account may not use the Assistant; disabled while the page cannot send. `List.tsx`, `List`, what a `/` or an `@` finds, rising from the composer's top line as wide as the conversation: rows in groups, a command, a Tile or a choice, what was typed marked, the row the user is on lit with an amber line, a line when nothing answers, the keys in a footer, said to a screen reader as it changes; under a finger, rows a thumb's height and no keys. `commands.ts`, the commands that take the place of a menu, on tiles, the agent and the user, the language among them, each a message, and `commandsMatching`, what a `/` finds, the language by its name in every language (`namesOf`), and `cutAround`, where what was typed shows in a text, tested (`commands.test.ts`). HEX-101 moves the commands beside the feature's `@` once it runs them; `List` and `cutAround` stay here. `demo.ts`, what the state hook will do on a real page, for the page alone, on a fixture System, its `@` by the rules of the feature's `mention.ts`. `gallery.tsx`, `ComposerGallery`, each state live on `/design-system` |
| `layouts/` | `Widget.tsx`, `Widget`, the frame of everything the conversation asks of the user or shows beyond words, a zone tinted by its `voice` (the agent's, the human's, the app's): its hex and line icon, a kicker, a title, a subtitle, a body, its actions (`Act`, the first in the voice, each key run while the focus is in it), and its tools on hover or focus, settings (`Menu`), fold (Radix's Collapsible) and dismiss. Its `state`: `waiting`, `working` (the hex breathes, light runs over the lines to come, `aria-busy`), `done` (one line, its receipt, with an undo), `failed` (the voice turns destructive). Folded or open is the user's. It comes in drawing its line, unfolding and raising its parts in turn, simply there under reduced motion. Below `sm` its hex shrinks; under a finger its tools fold into the one menu. `gallery.tsx`, `ChatLayoutsGallery`, on `/design-system` |
| `atoms/` | `Suggestions.tsx`, `Suggestions`, a list over the field it suggests for, which keeps the focus and the keys, a click in the field never closing it (`field.ts`, `inField`, tested), the field named a combobox controlling it by `suggestionsField`, the active suggestion kept in view, a click picking one; the composer's `@` search shows the Tiles in it. `gallery.tsx`, `ChatAtomsGallery`, on `/design-system` |

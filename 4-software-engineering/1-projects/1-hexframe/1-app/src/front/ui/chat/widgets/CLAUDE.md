# widgets

What used to be a drawer or a page and becomes a piece of the conversation, since hexframe is one screen (the grill of 2026-10-11, Q8): a tile's forms, Keys, who may see a tile (HEX-128), a star found (HEX-130), Help and signing in. Proposed (HEX-100; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-28` to `#DEC-32`), drawn on samples at `/design-system`; the builds wire them in: HEX-102 put the tile's in home's chat (`features/system/widget/`), HEX-103 the others (`features/keys/`, `features/shipped/`, `features/access/`). Each is controlled, as the `Composer` is: what it holds, what each field refuses and what its actions do are its caller's, a state hook's on a real page. `ui/` knows no domain: a widget mirrors a domain's words (a Branch, a Leaf, a Context Tile, the six Directions) and takes its rules as props (a Preview's limit, a Key name's), which the builds read from the domains. Once decided, a widget leaves the conversation's `Receipt` through `UntilDecided` (`entries/`), the focus handed to it. Wide, each of its rows that reaches the chat's lines' end runs on to the curve at its own height, the chat's gap short of it, past its widget's body where the curve bows out there (`toCurve`, `ui/layouts/`): a field and its line, a Key's row, a value to copy, Help's page a line at a time, a topic found, a shortcut's keys, an import's report line; a field beside another stays where it is.

`fields.tsx` stays the widgets' own: the builds hold the widgets' words in state hooks of their own rather than TanStack Form (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-39`, `#DEC-42`), and the shared `Field` and `useAppForm` are left to the client's channels' specimen and their own gallery, the Root's edit in the drawer gone to the tile widget.

# widgets

What used to be a drawer or a page and becomes a piece of the conversation, since hexframe is one screen (the grill of 2026-10-11, Q8): a tile's forms, Keys, Help and signing in. Proposed (HEX-100; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-28` to `#DEC-32`), drawn on samples at `/design-system`; the builds wire them in: HEX-102 put the tile's in home's chat (`features/system/widget/`), HEX-103 the others (`features/keys/`, `features/help/`, `features/access/`). Each is controlled, as the `Composer` is: what it holds, what each field refuses and what its actions do are its caller's, a state hook's on a real page. `ui/` knows no domain: a widget mirrors a domain's words (a Branch, a Leaf, a Context Tile, the six Directions) and takes its rules as props (a Preview's limit, a Key name's), which the builds read from the domains. Once decided, a widget leaves the conversation's `Receipt` through `UntilDecided` (`entries/`), the focus handed to it.

| Path | Holds |
|---|---|
| `tile/` | The tile widget. `TileWidget.tsx`, `TileWidget`: a new tile, an edit, or once written its receipt, in the user's amber; the title written where it is read (`TitleField`), the kind, the preview counted against the limit its caller gives, the page in the user's serif, written or read; its hex is the place (`PlaceMark`), the free places dashed and picked by a click or the arrows, by name under a finger; Create and Save are its submit, which Enter in a one-line field runs; an edit marks each field it changed, Delete asks first (`Dialog`); a tile changed meanwhile keeps the user's words, says who changed what, compares, and leaves Enter nothing to decide; a refused write comes back with the user's words and the refusal on the field. `Proposal.tsx`, `TileProposal`, the agent's draft of a new tile or its edit of one, the conversation's `Proposal` with the tile's place in its hex, its words in full or its edit where it falls, and Edit it. `Transfer.tsx`, `TileImport`, a vault into a free place or as the Root, its pickers its actions, working, then landed with what it left out, or refused with every fault; `TileExport`, working, then its receipt naming the zip, or failed. `parts.tsx`, what they share: a tile's words and their fields' names, its kinds, the places by name, where a tile stands, in words and drawn, a field read (`Read`), an edit where it falls (`Diff`, said to a screen reader). `fixtures.ts`, Tooling under Software engineering, the chat's sample tiles, every word a message. `gallery.tsx`, `TileWidgetGallery`, every state live, the Preview's limit handed over by the route |
| `keys/` | The key widget, the app's own, no voice. `KeyWidget.tsx`, `KeyWidget`: a name for the Key, counted against IAM's limit its caller gives, Create waiting while on its way, then the Key shown once, inking in (`animate-ink`), beside the command that uses it, each copied from its corner, one ember line saying it won't be shown again; the Keys given, a light on one used within the hour, each revoked after the `Dialog`; opened by the agent, the Keys alone, none issued; done, a receipt naming the Key, never its secret. `gallery.tsx`, `KeyWidgetGallery`, on sample Keys and a plainly fake secret |
| `help/` | The help widget, the app's own. `HelpWidget.tsx`, `HelpWidget`: a topic, its hex Help's own frame (`Places`), the topic lit and the map a way around, its page as written until the Markdown seam renders it, the topics it leads to as `Mention`s, Open in Help and, where an agent can be asked, Ask about it (`onAsk`, none signed out); what `/help` and a few words find, every topic counted, the first six shown; the shortcuts `?` shows (`shortcuts.ts`, proposals until TanStack Hotkeys comes, each said to a screen reader). `topics.ts`, Help's topics as the widget goes through them: the way around (`follow`, `topicsOf`, `placesTo`, `around`), where a topic leads as the specimen reads on (`readOnOf`), and `topicsFor`, the topics a search finds, a title first, each with the sentence it shows in cut around it by the composer's `cutAround`, all tested (`topics.test.ts`); they stay here, beside the gallery that reads them, and home's help widget reads them too (`features/help/`; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-43`). `gallery.tsx`, `HelpWidgetGallery`, on Help itself, which the route reads and hands over (`HelpNode`), since `ui/` can't |
| `sign-in/` | The sign-in widget. `SignInWidget.tsx`, `SignInWidget`: signed out, the chat's foot, on the composer's zone (`layouts/zone.ts`), the question in the agent's mono, an email and a password, the one solid act and the way between signing in and signing up, a refusal on the field at fault or under the act. `gallery.tsx`, `SignInWidgetGallery`, live, nothing reaching IAM; the signed-out `Screen`'s specimen shows it in place |

| File | Holds |
|---|---|
| `Places.tsx` | `Places`, a frame in small in a widget's hex's place: the tile in the middle, its six places around it numbered as the canvas numbers them, one lit in the voice, the free ones dashed; where a place may be picked, a radio group, the arrows going round and taking the one they land on. `namesByPlace`, a name for each place |
| `fields.tsx` | The widgets' fields, on one underline that draws itself in the voice as the focus comes and turns destructive while refused: `Field`, a labelled input or textarea, its count near its limit, its hint, a dot once changed, its refusal said as it changes; `TitleField`, a title at the widget's title size; `Choice`, a few words to choose among |
| `count.ts` | `countOf`, what a field with a limit shows of its length, from four fifths of it, ember for its last tenth, destructive past it, counted as a reader counts (`lengthOf`). Tested (`count.test.ts`) |
| `demo.ts` | What the widgets' state hooks will do on a real page, for the galleries alone, as the composer's `demo.ts` is: the places free for each kind, what Mapping and IAM would refuse before writing, against Mapping's Preview limit, which the route reads at its door, and IAM's rules, mirrored as IAM states them until HEX-115 brings them to its door, a refusal clearing from the field that changed, the fields an edit changed; never for a build. Tested (`demo.test.ts`) |

`fields.tsx` stays the widgets' own: the builds hold the widgets' words in state hooks of their own rather than TanStack Form (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-39`, `#DEC-42`), and the shared `Field` and `useAppForm` are left to the Root's edit in the drawer, their last user.

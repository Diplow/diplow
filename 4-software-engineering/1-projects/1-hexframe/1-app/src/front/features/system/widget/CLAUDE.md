# widget

Where a Tile is written on home since HEX-102: in the chat, at the conversation's foot, on ui's tile widget (`TileWidget`, `TileImport`, `TileExport`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/chat/widgets/CLAUDE|widgets]]). The route puts `TileWidgets` under the timeline and brings the chat to the front on a phone when a form opens, comes back refused, or an export starts.

- **The form is the URL's, what follows it the page's** (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-37`). Which form is open, a new Tile's in its slot, an edit or an import, is the change under way in the search params, as the drawer's was, so a link opens it and a refusal puts it back on the URL of its moment (`../state/useRefusalState.ts`). What follows a form is no change: the receipt a sent form leaves, or an export, working, then its receipt naming the zip, or failed with a retry. Home holds it (`state/useTileWidgetsState.ts`), so the card on the canvas and the chat share it. One at a time: a form opening clears it, a sent form or an export takes its place, and a receipt goes once its write is refused, so it never says done of a write that wasn't. While it shows, a receipt alone says its change: the state hook tells the route what it says (`told`, its Tile and the event Mapping names its write by), and the route hands that to the timeline, which leaves the change to it until it goes.
- **A form and its receipt are one widget.** A sent form's receipt is drawn by the same component, under the form's own key, so React keeps the one `TileWidget` and its hand-off moves the focus to the receipt. That is why a receipt carries its form's key and what the form opened on (`Session`).
- **What the user writes is a state hook, not TanStack Form** (`#DEC-39`). Mapping says what a Tile must be, through `decide`, foreseen or on the server. The widget's words and the refusal on each field are `useTileWordsState`'s, put back once per refusal turn and cleared field by field as the user changes them. The writes go through Mapping's form submits, which answer the Tile's id for the receipt.
- **No drawer.** The Root's edit is the tile widget's edit with no place, a pencil in its hex, since the Root stands under nothing (it replaced `RootDrawer` and the `Drawer`, `#DEC-38`'s stopgap). While the Root is untitled, and no form nor receipt is shown, the widget asks a new account its pseudo (`NameRootWidget`), which edits the Root's Title alone; what is typed is the page's (`useTileWidgetsState`'s `naming`), so the canvas shows it on the Root as it is typed, until a form opens. There is no Later: until the Root is named, home holds back its composer and the corner's path.
- **A new Tile's place is where the user clicked.** Its slot comes from the canvas, a Leaf's in a ring of Children, and the widget picks neither its kind nor its place; its hex shows its parent's six places, its own lit. Its import goes into the Branch's slot of the same Direction where it is free (`importSlotOf`).

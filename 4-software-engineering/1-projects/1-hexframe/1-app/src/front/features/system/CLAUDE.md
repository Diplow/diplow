# system

What home shows: the user's own System, read whole through `useSystem` (`front/client/mapping/queries.ts`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]]), with every write still on its way already on it, its tree drawn by the canvas, and changed from it. The route composes it with the breadcrumb, and passes both the System's Tiles as the canvas draws them. A Tile's forms, its import and its export are written in the chat, at the conversation's foot, as ui's tile widget (`TileWidget`, `TileImport`, `TileExport`; HEX-102): the route puts `widget/TileWidgets` under the timeline, in the screen's chat slot, and brings the chat to the front on a phone as one opens (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-37`).

- **Create**: a click on an empty slot opens the new Tile's widget for that slot: a Branch in a ring of Children or of Branches, a Leaf in a ring of Leaves, a Context Tile in a Context ring. Its hex is its parent's six places, the free ones dashed: another place, or another kind (a Branch, a Leaf, a Context Tile), picked there moves the change to that slot, what the user wrote kept. **Import here** turns it into an import into the same slot.
- **Import**: into an empty slot, a zip, a folder, picked or dropped, or one file, the only thing a Leaf slot takes; into an empty System, its untitled Root without Preview nor Body and with nothing below (Mapping's `isEmptySystem`, from its door), a vault, from **Import a vault** on the Root's card, and it becomes the Root. The browser leaves out what Mapping's reading would and says before sending what the server would refuse; the import's widget then reports the Tiles created and every file left out or skipped, and why, or, refused, every fault on its path, nothing written.
- **Edit, move, export, delete** act on the centered Tile, from the card beside the canvas. An edit opens the Tile's widget in the chat, each field changed marked, Save sending those, Delete asking first, counting the Tiles that go with it. The Root's edit stays in the drawer, on the shared form, since the widget draws no place for the Root (`#DEC-38`). Export saves the Tile and everything below it as `<slug>.zip`, the whole System from the Root, and the chat shows it, working, then its receipt naming the zip, or failed with a retry. The Root is the user, so it is only edited and exported. A move is a mode: the next empty slot clicked, anywhere the canvas is taken meanwhile, is where the Tile goes, of its own kind: a Leaf goes to a Leaf's slot, in a ring of Children or of Leaves, a Branch to a Branch's. Meanwhile every other Tile drawn where it stands offers "Swap with ⟨Title⟩" on a small button at the foot of its hex, and the two trade places; a Leaf and a Reference offer nothing, and neither do the Root, a broken Reference or a Tile above or below the moving one, which Mapping's `decide` refuses, and the Tile's own click still opens and centers it. A move or a swap ends as it is sent, and a refusal brings the move back, so another slot can be picked. A delete asks first, then centers the Tile it stood under at once.
- **Grow and shrink**: a Leaf's card offers **Make it a branch**, and a Branch's with nothing below it, no Child, no Context Tile nor Reference (Mapping's `holdsNothing`, from its door), **Make it a leaf**; a Branch holding anything isn't offered it, nor is the Root or a Context Tile. Each is a move to the same Direction of the other kind, Mapping having no operation of its own for it; a Tile of that kind already there refuses it `DirectionTaken`, in a toast. A Leaf that isn't Markdown, one an export writes as its content alone (Mapping's `isVerbatim`, through `api/mapping/files/download.ts`), shows its Body as code on its card, under its Title, its file's name.
- **A write shows at once.** The canvas and the card draw the System with every write still on its way on it, and the writes run in the order they were made, none waiting for the one before it to be answered. One button waits: a grow or a shrink, whose card offers the change back as soon as it shows, so a double click would undo it.
- **The change under way ends as its write is sent**, as if the write had landed: the widget becomes its receipt, the focus handed to it, a move or a swap ends, a delete centers the Tile it stood under. The receipt stays at the conversation's foot until another form opens or an export starts; a form closed unsent leaves nothing. **A refusal puts it back** (`state/useRefusalState.ts`), on the URL of the moment it arrives: a refused create or edit reopens its widget with what the user typed and the refusal on the field it names, cleared as that field changes, a refused move or swap brings the move back. A refused delete puts nothing back: the Tile shows again where it stood, the System no longer folding the write, and the view stays where it is; nor does a refused grow or shrink, which was no change in the URL. A change the user has under way meanwhile, a form or a move, is theirs: the refusal leaves it be. There, and off the page, a refusal shows in a toast alone, a field's included.
- **Errors take their channel**: a refusal on a form's field (`TitleMissing`, `PreviewTooLong`) shows under that field, the form reopened, an import's (`ImportRefused`, on its files) in its widget; any other (`MovedUnderItself`, `DirectionTaken`, `TileNotFound`, `RootFixed`, and `TileChanged`, a write over a Tile changed since the page drew it, in another tab or by an agent, a refused edit's form reopened with what the user typed) shows in a toast. Nothing here catches one.
- **Each write names the Version the page drew** of every Tile it changes, read from the flat System the page shows: the card's edit, delete, grow and shrink from the centered Tile, the canvas's move and swap through `moveOf` and `swapOf`.

## Rules

- **The change under way belongs to the URL**, like the view: a link opens the same form, or the same move. The tile widget is the open drawer it replaced, so STACK's state table keeps it there; what follows it, a receipt or an export, is no change and lives in the page (`#DEC-37`). It holds one change at most; `withChange` replaces it, a view change keeps it, and so does a delete, unless the change named the deleted Tile or one below it. A write ends its change as it is sent; what a refusal puts back applies to the URL of the moment it arrives, not the one the write was sent from.
- **No component handles a write's settle.** A component sends its write and changes the URL at once; `useRefusalState`, the one state hook that reads the refused writes, puts a change back. It is called once, by `TileWidgets`, where the forms are.
- **The form speaks Mapping's words**: a Title, a Preview, a Body. An edit sends only the fields that changed, so the Root's Body can be written before its name.

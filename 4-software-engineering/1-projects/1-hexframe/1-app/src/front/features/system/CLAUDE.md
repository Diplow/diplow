# system

What home shows: the user's own System, read whole through `useSystem` (`front/client/mapping/queries.ts`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]]), with every write still on its way already on it, its tree drawn by the canvas, and changed from it. The route composes it with the breadcrumb, and passes both the System's Tiles as the canvas draws them. A Tile's forms, its import and its export are written in the chat, at the conversation's foot, as ui's tile widget (`TileWidget`, `TileImport`, `TileExport`; HEX-102): the route puts `widget/TileWidgets` under the timeline, in the screen's chat slot, and brings the chat to the front on a phone as one opens (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-37`).

- **Create**: a click on an empty slot opens the new Tile's widget for that slot, where the user clicked saying what it is, since a new Tile is a Leaf: a Leaf in a ring of Children or of Leaves, a Branch past six Children, in a ring of Branches, a Context Tile in a Context ring. Its hex is its parent's six places, its own lit; no kind nor place is picked there. Its title alone, beside the hex, takes the focus; Enter there brings the preview, Enter in the preview the page, both the widget's whole width; ⌘↵ or Create sends it at any point. **Import here** turns it into an import into the same Direction, a Branch's slot where it is free, since a folder takes one. A Leaf becomes a Branch when its owner adds a Tile under it: its menu's Add a tile grows it first, in the System's queue before the new Tile's create (`turnOf`), where Mapping lets it.
- **A new account's pseudo**: while the Root is untitled, the chat asks, in the agent's voice, the pseudo the user goes by, its field focused, which names the Root, so their own tile carries it at once, the untitled Root's empty hex showing it as it is typed (`canvasTree`'s `naming`), its warm light small until it is taken, the composer and the corner's path held back meanwhile, all of it arriving in turn as the pseudo is taken, from the corner down: the path coming down from the mark, the light rising, the question leaving its receipt, the composer rising (ui's dawn). It is there to be answered, with no tools and no Later; an empty System may import a vault instead. `/design-system` plays it again on the same pieces, its Onboarding slide (`ui/gallery/deck/tour.tsx`), which follows what home does here.
- **Import**: into an empty slot, a zip, a folder, picked or dropped, or one file, the only thing a Leaf slot takes, from the slot's menu or its new Tile's widget; into an empty System, its untitled Root without Preview nor Body and with nothing below (Mapping's `isEmptySystem`, from its door), a vault, from **Import a vault** in the Root's menu, and it becomes the Root, which its preview invites. The browser leaves out what Mapping's reading would and says before sending what the server would refuse; the import's widget then reports the Tiles created and every file left out or skipped, and why, or, refused, every fault on its path, nothing written.
- **A Tile's menu** (HEX-105's `TileMenu`, on home since HEX-107; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-47`): by a right click, a long press, ⇧F10 or the menu key, each line's key running on the focused Tile too. Its lines, by kind and place (ui's `groupsOf`): edit, which opens the Tile's widget in the chat, the Root's too, its hex a pencil since it stands under nothing (no drawer is left); make it a Leaf or a Branch, where Mapping's `decide` allows it; add a Tile at a place of the frame it holds, a Leaf's empty, growing it into a Branch, which opens the new Tile's widget, its title taking the focus as the menu lets it go; move it to a free place of its ring, or swap it with a Tile there, from the frame it stands in, each place offering what `decide` would make of it, or "Somewhere else…", the move on the canvas; copy its link, its title or its mention; share it, which opens who may see it in the chat, its share widget, the user's own Tiles alone, never a Reference (`share`, HEX-128); export it, which the chat shows, working, then its receipt naming the zip, or failed with a retry; delete it, which asks first in the `Dialog`, counting the Tiles that go with it; a Reference's removal, its Tile kept. Open, Go in, Show its Context and Back out are the canvas's. The center neither moves nor goes, being where you are; the Root is the user, so it is only named, edited, exported and, an empty System's, imported into. The agent's line asks through the chat (HEX-110): "Ask the agent about it" names the Tile first in the composer, what was written kept, the focus just after it, and a free slot's "Ask the agent for one" names the Tile it would go under and the place, both told to the chat as an `AskedAboutTile` fact (`features/facts.ts`); a Leaf's Context is left out, which it has none of, and the agent's line on a broken Reference, which names no Tile. A free slot's menu opens its new Tile's widget or an import into it. Every Tile shows its hover card, its place, title, preview and a note, under the pointer or on keyboard focus.
- **A move** is under way in the URL (`move`): started by a mouse dragging a Tile its menu lets move, or by "Somewhere else…". The Tile lifts, its rim running; every free slot that takes it, of its own kind, offers itself, "Here" under the pointer, and every other Tile drawn where it stands that trades places with it offers its swap on a disc at its foot; a Leaf and a Reference offer nothing, and neither do the Root, a broken Reference or a Tile above or below the moving one, which Mapping's `decide` refuses. The rest steps back, its click still opening and centering, so the canvas can be taken anywhere meanwhile. Let go over a place, or a place or a disc clicked, the Tile glides there for `--duration-glide` and its write is sent, ending the move; refused, it flares where it went, glides back, and the move is under way again. Escape or the banner's Stop, at the canvas's foot, ends it. Dragged off the canvas, the Tile is carried over the page (`Carried`), the route handing it to the composer, which lights to take it and names it at the caret where it is let go; let go off the canvas it lands nowhere and the move ends (HEX-110, `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-50`). A finger never drags: it moves a Tile from its menu.
- **Grow and shrink**: a Leaf's menu offers **Make it a Branch**, and a Branch's **Make it a Leaf**, where Mapping's `decide` makes the move to the same Direction of the other kind, Mapping having no operation of its own for it: not for a Branch holding anything, nor where a Tile of the other kind stands in that Direction, nor for the Root or a Context Tile. A refusal the server still makes shows in a toast. A Leaf that isn't Markdown, one an export writes as its content alone (Mapping's `isVerbatim`, through `api/mapping/files/download.ts`), shows its file's name as its Title, in mono; its Body, which the centered Tile's card showed as code before the card went, shows in its widget's edit until a piece of the canvas shows it.
- **A write shows at once.** The canvas draws the System with every write still on its way on it, and the writes run in the order they were made, none waiting for the one before it to be answered. On the canvas, a Tile a write on its way changes says so, a light sweeping over it once 400ms pass without an answer, a free slot an import lands in sweeps, and a Tile whose write was refused flares as it is put back (`state/useWritesState.ts`). One line waits: a grow or a shrink, whose menu offers the change back as soon as it shows, so a second one would undo it.
- **The change under way ends as its write is sent**, as if the write had landed: the widget becomes its receipt, the focus handed to it, a move or a swap ends, a delete, from the widget or the menu, centers the Tile it stood under, and ends a change under way that named it. The receipt stays at the conversation's foot until another form opens, an export starts or its write is refused; a form closed unsent leaves nothing. **A refusal puts it back** (`state/useRefusalState.ts`), on the URL of the moment it arrives: a refused create or edit reopens its widget with what the user typed and the refusal on the field it names, cleared as that field changes, a refused move or swap brings the move back. A refused delete puts nothing back: the Tile shows again where it stood, the System no longer folding the write, and the view stays where it is; nor does a refused grow or shrink, which was no change in the URL. A change the user has under way meanwhile, a form or a move, is theirs: the refusal leaves it be. There, and off the page, a refusal shows in a toast alone, a field's included.
- **Errors take their channel**: a refusal on a form's field (`TitleMissing`, `PreviewTooLong`) shows under that field, the form reopened, an import's (`ImportRefused`, on its files) in its widget; any other (`MovedUnderItself`, `DirectionTaken`, `TileNotFound`, `RootFixed`, and `TileChanged`, a write over a Tile changed since the page drew it, in another tab or by an agent, a refused edit's form reopened with what the user typed) shows in a toast. Nothing here catches one.
- **A visit** (HEX-127): a link to a Tile of someone else's System, `/t/<id>`, lands on home with `system`, their star, in the URL (`visit/visit.ts`), the view's fields its own meanwhile: the canvas draws their System as the Account may see it (`useSystem`'s `star`), from their star's card, each Tile they may read below one kept from them hung from the star across a gap (`visitTree`, the canvas's `gaps` and `across`), which the corner draws as a "…". The same `System` draws it, looked at, not acted on, for now (`Reach` `looking`): a Tile's menu keeps the lines that go somewhere and Copy, and no free slot, move or line that acts is offered, whatever its Grants give the visitor, since a write the page sends acts on the Account's own System until HEX-132 and a Turn starts at home alone until HEX-134 (`hexframe-app-sharing-organizations/decisions.md#DEC-4`). The chat names their Tiles as it names the Account's (`titlesIn`).
- **Each write names the Version the page drew** of every Tile it changes, read from the flat System the page shows: a menu's delete, grow, shrink, move, swap and a Reference's removal, the canvas's move and swap, through `moveOf` and `swapOf`.

## Rules

- **The change under way belongs to the URL**, like the view: a link opens the same form, or the same move. The tile widget is the open drawer it replaced, so STACK's state table keeps it there; what follows it, a receipt or an export, is no change and lives in the page (`#DEC-37`). It holds one change at most; `withChange` replaces it, a view change keeps it, and so does a delete, unless the change named the deleted Tile or one below it. A write ends its change as it is sent; what a refusal puts back applies to the URL of the moment it arrives, not the one the write was sent from.
- **No component handles a write's settle.** A component sends its write and changes the URL at once; `useRefusalState`, the one state hook that puts a change back, is called once, by `TileWidgets`, where the forms are. `useWritesState` reads the refused writes too, only to flare their Tiles, and leaves whether the page shows a refusal to the others.
- **The form speaks Mapping's words**: a Title, a Preview, a Body. An edit sends only the fields that changed, so the Root's Body can be written before its name.

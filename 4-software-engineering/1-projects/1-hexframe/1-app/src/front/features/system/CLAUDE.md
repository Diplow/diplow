# system

What home shows: the user's own System, read whole through `useSystem` (`front/client/mapping/queries.ts`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]]), with every write still on its way already on it, its tree drawn by the canvas, and changed from it. The route composes it with the breadcrumb, and passes both the System's Tiles as the canvas draws them. A Tile's forms, its import and its export are written in the chat, at the conversation's foot, as ui's tile widget (`TileWidget`, `TileImport`, `TileExport`; HEX-102): the route puts `widget/TileWidgets` under the timeline, in the screen's chat slot, and brings the chat to the front on a phone as one opens (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-37`).

- **Create**: a click on an empty slot opens the new Tile's widget for that slot: a Branch in a ring of Children or of Branches, a Leaf in a ring of Leaves, a Context Tile in a Context ring. Its hex is its parent's six places, the free ones dashed: another place, or another kind (a Branch, a Leaf, a Context Tile), picked there moves the change to that slot, what the user wrote kept. **Import here** turns it into an import into the same slot.
- **Import**: into an empty slot, a zip, a folder, picked or dropped, or one file, the only thing a Leaf slot takes, from the slot's menu or its new Tile's widget; into an empty System, its untitled Root without Preview nor Body and with nothing below (Mapping's `isEmptySystem`, from its door), a vault, from **Import a vault** on the Root's card or its menu, and it becomes the Root. The browser leaves out what Mapping's reading would and says before sending what the server would refuse; the import's widget then reports the Tiles created and every file left out or skipped, and why, or, refused, every fault on its path, nothing written.
- **A Tile's menu** (HEX-105's `TileMenu`, on home since HEX-107; `hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-47`): by a right click, a long press, ⇧F10 or the menu key, each line's key running on the focused Tile too. Its lines, by kind and place (ui's `groupsOf`): edit, which opens the Tile's widget in the chat (the Root's edit stays in the drawer, `#DEC-38`); make it a Leaf or a Branch, where Mapping's `decide` allows it; add a Tile at a place of the frame it holds, which opens the new Tile's widget; move it to a free place of its ring, or swap it with a Tile there, from the frame it stands in, each place offering what `decide` would make of it, or "Somewhere else…", the move on the canvas; copy its link, its title or its mention; export it, which the chat shows, working, then its receipt naming the zip, or failed with a retry; delete it, which asks first in the `Dialog`, counting the Tiles that go with it; a Reference's removal, its Tile kept. Open, Go in, Show its Context and Back out are the canvas's. The center neither moves nor goes, being where you are; the Root is the user, so it is only named, edited, exported and, an empty System's, imported into. The agent's line is left out until the chat answers the menu (HEX-110), and so is a Leaf's Context, which it has none of. A free slot's menu opens its new Tile's widget or an import into it. Every Tile shows its hover card, its place, title, preview and a note, under the pointer or on keyboard focus.
- **A move** is under way in the URL (`move`): started by a mouse dragging a Tile its menu lets move, or by "Somewhere else…". The Tile lifts, its rim running; every free slot that takes it, of its own kind, offers itself, "Here" under the pointer, and every other Tile drawn where it stands that trades places with it offers its swap on a disc at its foot; a Leaf and a Reference offer nothing, and neither do the Root, a broken Reference or a Tile above or below the moving one, which Mapping's `decide` refuses. The rest steps back, its click still opening and centering, so the canvas can be taken anywhere meanwhile. Let go over a place, or a place or a disc clicked, the Tile glides there for `--duration-glide` and its write is sent, ending the move; refused, it flares where it went, glides back, and the move is under way again. Escape or the banner's Stop, at the canvas's foot, ends it. A finger never drags: it moves a Tile from its menu.
- **Grow and shrink**: a Leaf's menu offers **Make it a Branch**, and a Branch's **Make it a Leaf**, where Mapping's `decide` makes the move to the same Direction of the other kind, Mapping having no operation of its own for it: not for a Branch holding anything, nor where a Tile of the other kind stands in that Direction, nor for the Root or a Context Tile. A refusal the server still makes shows in a toast. A Leaf that isn't Markdown, one an export writes as its content alone (Mapping's `isVerbatim`, through `api/mapping/files/download.ts`), shows its Body as code on the centered Tile's card, under its Title, its file's name.
- **A write shows at once.** The canvas and the card draw the System with every write still on its way on it, and the writes run in the order they were made, none waiting for the one before it to be answered. On the canvas, a Tile a write on its way changes says so, a light sweeping over it once 400ms pass without an answer, a free slot an import lands in sweeps, and a Tile whose write was refused flares as it is put back (`state/useWritesState.ts`). One line waits: a grow or a shrink, whose menu offers the change back as soon as it shows, so a second one would undo it.
- **The change under way ends as its write is sent**, as if the write had landed: the widget becomes its receipt, the focus handed to it, a move or a swap ends, a delete, from the widget or the menu, centers the Tile it stood under, and ends a change under way that named it. The receipt stays at the conversation's foot until another form opens, an export starts or its write is refused; a form closed unsent leaves nothing. **A refusal puts it back** (`state/useRefusalState.ts`), on the URL of the moment it arrives: a refused create or edit reopens its widget with what the user typed and the refusal on the field it names, cleared as that field changes, a refused move or swap brings the move back. A refused delete puts nothing back: the Tile shows again where it stood, the System no longer folding the write, and the view stays where it is; nor does a refused grow or shrink, which was no change in the URL. A change the user has under way meanwhile, a form or a move, is theirs: the refusal leaves it be. There, and off the page, a refusal shows in a toast alone, a field's included.
- **Errors take their channel**: a refusal on a form's field (`TitleMissing`, `PreviewTooLong`) shows under that field, the form reopened, an import's (`ImportRefused`, on its files) in its widget; any other (`MovedUnderItself`, `DirectionTaken`, `TileNotFound`, `RootFixed`, and `TileChanged`, a write over a Tile changed since the page drew it, in another tab or by an agent, a refused edit's form reopened with what the user typed) shows in a toast. Nothing here catches one.
- **Each write names the Version the page drew** of every Tile it changes, read from the flat System the page shows: a menu's delete, grow, shrink, move, swap and a Reference's removal, the canvas's move and swap, through `moveOf` and `swapOf`.

## Rules

- **The change under way belongs to the URL**, like the view: a link opens the same form, or the same move. The tile widget is the open drawer it replaced, so STACK's state table keeps it there; what follows it, a receipt or an export, is no change and lives in the page (`#DEC-37`). It holds one change at most; `withChange` replaces it, a view change keeps it, and so does a delete, unless the change named the deleted Tile or one below it. A write ends its change as it is sent; what a refusal puts back applies to the URL of the moment it arrives, not the one the write was sent from.
- **No component handles a write's settle.** A component sends its write and changes the URL at once; `useRefusalState`, the one state hook that puts a change back, is called once, by `TileWidgets`, where the forms are. `useWritesState` reads the refused writes too, only to flare their Tiles, and leaves whether the page shows a refusal to the others.
- **The form speaks Mapping's words**: a Title, a Preview, a Body. An edit sends only the fields that changed, so the Root's Body can be written before its name.

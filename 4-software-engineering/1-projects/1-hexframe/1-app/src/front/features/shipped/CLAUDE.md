# shipped

What home shows of the Systems hexframe ships, each read whole through `useShipped` in the page's language (`front/client/mapping/queries.ts`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]]) and drawn by the canvas as one System is (`ShippedCanvas`). Help (HEX-103) is read in the help widget too (`HelpWidget`, [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/chat/widgets/CLAUDE|widgets]]): signed out, it is always on the canvas; signed in, it takes the canvas while the widget is open. The tutorial (HEX-117) takes the canvas while the URL names it, signed in, which `/tutorial` sets, on its Root, a new account invited to it by the agent's line after its dawn (HEX-118, `features/conversation/`), the canvas journeying there from the user's sun (HEX-119, the route's `Journey`), where the agent tells its center's words in turn, its title lit, then its preview, then its body in the chat (HEX-120), after which its center invites the click that opens it to its six (HEX-121), its body's lines and its six shown as the same six (HEX-122), where it stops for now; its later steps are the project's next tickets ("hexframe app: Tutorial" in Linear). The route composes both with the breadcrumb, and passes their Tiles as the canvas draws them (`canvasTree`, from the system feature). This was Help's feature, `features/help/`, until the tutorial came (`hexframe-app-tutorial/decisions.md#DEC-3`).

- **Read-only.** No empty slot takes a click and no Tile offers a swap: the canvas only changes the view, as on home; each Tile's menu holds the lines that go somewhere, Open, Go in, Show its Context and Back out, beside its hover card (the canvas's `looking`, never `acting`), so Help's Context is a right click away.
- **A topic of Help opens in the chat.** Help's Root stands alone and large on the canvas, its Preview whole, until a click opens it, and a topic centered lands with its own around it, one with none alone and large; signed out, the agent offers to read it in full under its two lines (`HelpRead`), which opens its page in the help widget, where the centered Tile's card and its Read were; signed in, `/help` opens it, on the topic the words after it find. While the widget is open, a topic centered on the canvas turns its page; the map turns the page, a topic it reads on and Open in Help center the canvas on it (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-43`).
- **The tutorial is away from home.** `shippedOnCanvas` says so (`away`): the route keys the canvas's `Journey` by it and gives the corner's mark the way back; Help opens over the user's System as a widget's page, and swaps.
- **The tutorial stands where its param puts it.** Its Root alone and large for `true`, else the Tile it names, the one field that says where the tutorial is centered: a move on its canvas writes the Tile centered there and leaves the view's `center`, the System's, empty. Help's widget open takes the canvas from it.
- **The tutorial has its own rules: what it has not introduced is absent** (HEX-119). `tutorial/rules.ts` says, from the view the URL asks for, the view it shows and what it lets through, its center's click, the menus, moving the center, the composer, a Context introduced by no step, and `shippedOnCanvas` applies it, so the canvas (`ShippedCanvas`'s `rules`) and the chat (the composer held back, as during the onboarding) read one answer. At its arrival nothing goes through until the agent has told the center's body, then its center's click alone (HEX-121); a link that names a Tile below it, a Branch open or a Context shows the center as `/tutorial` does, and one that names its ring lands on the center opened; each later step lifts exactly what it introduces. No progress is kept: the step follows from the view, and `/tutorial` again sets it on its Root (`hexframe-app-tutorial/decisions.md#DEC-8`).
- **The tutorial's center tells its words in turn** (HEX-120). While the tutorial says so (`shippedTells`, from the URL alone, on `tutorialTells`), at its arrival and once opened, the route plays the beats, one every `--stagger-beat` once the tutorial is read (`useTold`, `ui/motion.ts`), and again from the start each time it arrives anew: the agent's few words in the chat, and on the canvas the part they name lit (`ShippedCanvas`'s `told`), its title, then its preview, then, with "And a body.", the body itself in the chat, read from the tutorial's Tile, its numbered list a list (`Told`, `ui/chat/text/`), a phone bringing the chat to the front for it. No progress is kept here either: the beats follow from the view (`#DEC-10`), and the body stays told once the center opens.
- **The tutorial's center opens to its six** (HEX-121). Once its body is told, the rules let the center's click through (`shippedOnCanvas` takes what was told), and the canvas introduces it (`introducesOpening`): the center, the one thing that answers, invites the click without a word, its rim breathing in your amber, and a click, Enter or a tap opens it, its six arriving one by one from the top left, `--stagger-step` apart, all at once with reduced motion. Opened, each Tile shows its hover card alone, its title and its preview, both told already, no menu and no click, the center's click folding it back (`#DEC-12`, `#DEC-13`).
- **The tutorial's body and its ring are the same six** (HEX-122). Opened, its rules say so (`sameSix`): each numbered line of the center's body names the Child in the Direction its number says (`tutorial/ring.ts`'s `ringOf`), and as the six land, one by one, each lights its line, the lines lit until a flare after the last (`useAround`, `ui/motion.ts`); then a line hovered or focused lights its tile on the canvas, in your amber as the chat's pointing does (`marks`' `pointed`), a tile hovered or focused lights its line, its card open, or its menu's header under a finger's long press (the canvas's `onPoint`), and a line clicked or tapped points at its tile, a phone bringing the canvas forward for a glance. A line that names no Child lights nothing, nor does the center. The agent then says "The same six.", and a beat later "Yours is waiting.", the way back already in the corner: the six can't be entered yet, each a later project's chapter. `sameSixOf` says once what the canvas and the chat take of it, and `useSameSix` (`tutorial/six.ts`) plays it, from the start each time the center opens anew, the tile pointed at let go as it folds (`#DEC-14`, `#DEC-15`).

## Rules

- **The view and where each is open belong to the URL**, as on home: a link opens Help or the tutorial where its sender was, Help's widget included. A shipped System's ids are the same in every language, so switching languages keeps both.
- **Anyone reads them.** Their reads ask for no Account (`hexframe-app-mcp-server/decisions.md#DEC-19`, `#DEC-20`).
- **Leaving one gives the canvas back to the System.** While Help has the canvas, the view's fields are its own; while the tutorial has it, its param holds the center and the view's other fields are its own. So whatever takes the canvas back takes them with it, and no Tile of Help or of the tutorial is left for the System to center: Help's Dismiss lands on the user's Root, as a link without the param does; a Tile the chat names centers the System on it; `/help` over the tutorial gives the canvas to Help, whose Dismiss lands on the Root, and `/tutorial` over Help closes its widget, the tutorial taking the canvas on its Root. From the tutorial, the corner's mark is the way back (HEX-119): it gives the canvas back to the user's Root, the composer returning, the canvas journeying home (`#DEC-9`).

# conversation

What the chat beside the canvas shows: one timeline per Account, a day at a time, newest at the bottom beside the composer, drawn with the chat's pieces (ui's `chat/`). Home lays it out on the one screen over the Account's own Conversation, the timeline beside the curve and the composer at the chat's foot (`ChatTimeline`, `ChatComposer`); `/design-system` lays out the same two parts together, on fixtures (`Conversation`, `gallery.tsx`).

The user's words come in their serif behind an amber line, the agent's in mono behind its violet hex, a run of the agent's marked on its first only. What happened between them goes on a thread, one line each, coloured by who acted: a change and an import by the user, the agent, a Key or a revoked one; a navigation, its last gesture in the canvas's words; a Turn's end, a Stop by the user, a limit or a failure by hexframe. A Tile named shows the Title it had, centering the canvas while the System holds it, the agent's draft while a Proposal not accepted creates it, struck through once deleted; a Message's link shows its Tile's Title now. In ask the agent's changes come as one Proposal, which the user accepts whole or discards, asked first, and which then leaves its receipt; in apply they land as it goes, and once the Turn ends a receipt says how many it applied. Both receipts offer an Undo, once. After the last Entry come the user's Messages still on their way, or refused with a retry, and, while a Turn runs, the agent's step breathing on the thread, or that it thinks.

The user writes in the composer, `@`s a Tile, and the Message keeps it by its id. A `/` opening the field lists the commands home offers: `/language`, which lists the languages and goes to the one picked, a plain link and a full load; `/ask` and `/apply`, the Mode, while it shows; `/undo`, the latest the agent applied that still offers its Undo, while there is one (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-33`). A Message the user sends goes through the route, which posts it after the navigation it follows, from the view it was sent from, and a Turn answers it, in the Tile the Message opens with a link to, else the centered one; while it runs the field waits and send becomes Stop. The Mode shows under the field, or the line saying the Account may not use the Assistant. The chat says "the agent" (`#DEC-34`); what it drew where the proposals drew nothing is `#DEC-35`.

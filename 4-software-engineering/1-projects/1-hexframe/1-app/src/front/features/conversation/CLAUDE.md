# conversation

What the chat beside the canvas shows: one timeline per Account, a day at a time, newest at the bottom beside the composer, drawn with the chat's pieces (ui's `chat/`). Home lays it out on the one screen over the Account's own Conversation, the timeline beside the curve and the composer at the chat's foot (`ChatTimeline`, `ChatComposer`); `/design-system` lays out the same two parts together, on fixtures (`Conversation`, `gallery.tsx`).

The user's words come in their serif behind an amber line, the agent's in mono behind its violet hex, a run of the agent's marked on its first only. What happened between them goes on a thread, one line each, coloured by who acted: a change and an import by the user, the agent, a Key or a revoked one; a navigation, its last gesture in the canvas's words; a Turn's end, a Stop by the user, a limit or a failure by hexframe. A Tile named shows the Title it had, centering the canvas while the System holds it, with its card, which the route reads off the System and the open Proposal as the canvas's card says it, and, hovered or focused, lights its Tile on home's canvas (HEX-109), the agent's draft while a Proposal not accepted creates it, struck through once deleted; a Message's link shows its Tile's Title now. In ask the agent's changes come as one Proposal, which the user accepts whole or discards, asked first, and which then leaves its receipt; in apply they land as it goes, and once the Turn ends a receipt says how many it applied. Both receipts offer an Undo, once. A change the user sent from a tile widget's form, the pseudo taken included, its receipt says at the conversation's foot: the timeline leaves it to the receipt while it shows, and tells it once it goes, so the two never say the same thing one above the other. After the last Entry come the user's Messages still on their way, or refused with a retry, where the user went that the Conversation doesn't hold yet, each navigation sent and the one under way, which each gesture joins, told as the timeline tells a navigation, in the order they were sent (HEX-110), and, while a Turn runs, the agent's step breathing on the thread, or that it thinks.

The user writes in the composer, `@`s a Tile, and the Message keeps it by its id. A `/` opening the field lists the commands home offers: `/language`, which lists the languages and goes to the one picked, a plain link and a full load; `/ask` and `/apply`, the Mode, while it shows; `/undo`, the latest the agent applied that still offers its Undo, while there is one (`hexframe-app-design-system-v1/decisions.md#DEC-33`); `/keys`, the Account's Keys in the chat; `/tutorial` (`/tutoriel` in French), the tutorial on the canvas, at its Root, which the route answers with the tutorial's param (HEX-118); `/logout`, which signs out on this device, then loads home afresh, signed out; and `/help`, Help on the canvas and in the chat, on the topic the words after it find, which the header's links opened before the header went. Nothing said yet, the timeline shows nothing; but a new account is invited, a line of the conversation rather than a box: while its Root, named, holds nothing below it and nothing was said, no Message held nor one on its way, the agent's one line after the receipt at the conversation's foot says "Another system is near. Type /tutorial to reach it.", the command in the user's amber, which a click runs as typing it does, and a `/` lists `/tutorial` first. It is derived, never stored, so it goes once a Tile stands below the Root or something is said; the change that named the Root says nothing, nor does a navigation (`hexframe-app-tutorial/decisions.md#DEC-4`). Under the pseudo's receipt, it rises fifth of the dawn, once the composer has. A Message the user sends goes through the route, which posts it after the navigation it follows, from the view it was sent from, and a Turn answers it, in the Tile the Message opens with a link to, else the centered one, which the composer says under its field as it is written (Assistant's `turnLocation`); while it runs the field waits and send becomes Stop. A Tile a menu on the canvas asks the agent about is named first in the field, what was written kept, the focus just after it, a free slot's place written after its Tile; a Tile carried off the canvas is named at the caret where it is let go over the composer (HEX-110). The Mode shows under the field, and nothing does for an Account that may not use the Assistant. The chat says "the agent" (`#DEC-34`); what it drew where the proposals drew nothing is `#DEC-35`.

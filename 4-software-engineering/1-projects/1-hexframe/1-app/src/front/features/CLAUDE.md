# features

A feature is client code that shows one thing a page needs, in a domain's language, built from [[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/ui/CLAUDE|ui]]. A route composes features: home lays out, on the one screen (`Screen`, `ui/layouts/`), the user's own System on the canvas, its center alone and large until a click opens it, the breadcrumb in its corner, and the chat over the Account's Conversation, its timeline and its composer in the screen's two slots, and publishes a `Navigated` for every gesture that changes the view, on the canvas or the breadcrumb; what the chat points at, centers and proposes crosses to the canvas through the route (HEX-109): a Tile named or the `@` list's row lighting its Tile, a Tile centered glinting and, on a phone, glancing, the agent's light where its Turn works, what the agent or a Key changed since the page opened marked until opened, and the open Proposal standing on the canvas as ghosts, rims and arcs; and from the canvas to the chat (HEX-110): under the composer's field, where the next Turn works, the centered Tile unless the Message opens with a link; a Tile's menu asking the agent, its Tile named first in the composer through an `AskedAboutTile` fact; each gesture joining the navigation under way, which the timeline shows as it goes and as it is sent; and a Tile dragged off the canvas under a mouse or a pen carried over the page, the composer naming it where it is let go; the Keys and Help open in the chat, Help taking the canvas, the tutorial taking it too while the URL names it, which `/tutorial` sets, the canvas journeying there, the tutorial by its own rules and the corner's mark the way back (HEX-119), a new account invited to it by the agent's line after its dawn (HEX-118), and signed out the screen shows Help and the sign-in widget; `/design-system` shows the Conversation and the breadcrumb on fixtures, a Message's link centering the canvas there.

## Rules

- **A feature sits between routes and the client's side of the API.** dependency-cruiser reads `front/routes/` → `front/features/` → `front/client/` and `front/ui/` ([[4-software-engineering/1-projects/1-hexframe/1-app/src/front/CLAUDE|front]]): a feature reaches the server through a server function, never a repository, and of a domain only its door, pure (`entities/index.ts`, `operations/index.ts`, `errors.ts`).
- **Features ignore each other.** A route composes them, and the client bus carries a fact from one to another; `no-feature-importing-another` says no to the import.
- **A fact is declared once, with its schema, where both sides reach it**, as a `Schema.TaggedClass` in the past tense and in a domain's language: in `facts.ts` beside `bus.ts`, created with the first fact, never inside the feature that publishes it, which its listener may not import.
- **A feature reacts through a state hook**: `useFact(Navigated, actions.recordNavigation)`, passing a stable action, so the hook subscribes once. React subscribes through `useSyncExternalStore`, since `useEffect` stays in `ui/`.
- **A fact crossing into the client is decoded by its schema**, through `receive`, which drops and reports one that does not decode; between features, in one page, its type is enough. Every fact is logged at `medium`, by its tag (`log('bus', …)` from the API layer's observability), and a feature that throws reacting to one is reported to Sentry.
- **A feature builds from ui/ and never adds to it.** A missing component is a Linear ticket (see ui's rules).
- **The view belongs to the URL**, as on the canvas: a feature that changes it calls `onViewChange` with the next view and the gesture that asked for it, on which Tile (`ViewAction`); the route navigates, and home publishes the gesture as a `Navigated`.

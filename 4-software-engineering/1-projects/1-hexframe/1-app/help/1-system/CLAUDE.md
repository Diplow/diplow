A System is the whole hierarchy you maintain. Each account has exactly one.

Its **Root** is you. There is no profile beside it: the Root's Title is your name everywhere in the app, and it starts untitled until you name yourself. The Root can be edited like any Tile, but never moved, swapped nor deleted.

Everything you lay out hangs below the Root: the codebase you work on, the team you lead, the parts of your life you want an agent to understand. One System holds them all, so one reader can see how they relate.

*Hexframe* is the product and the form. What you own is a System.

Help, this guide, is a System too, one hexframe ships with: every user reads it, nobody owns it, nobody writes it.

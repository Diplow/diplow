Below a Tile sit its **Children**, which say what the Tile does and how. Each stands in one of six **Directions** around it, numbered clockwise from the top left:

| Direction | Where |
|---|---|
| 1 | NW |
| 2 | NE |
| 3 | E |
| 4 | SE |
| 5 | SW |
| 6 | W |

The **Opposite** of a Direction is three away: 1 and 4, 2 and 5, 3 and 6. Two Children across from each other are a tension the parent balances, like speed and safety, or building and maintaining. Placing them that way says so.

A Tile together with its Children is a **Frame**: what you see when you open it.

A Tile holds six Children at most, and a seventh is refused. Its Context says why.

# Legacy

wikipol's vaults from before its Sources became nodes under [[6-politics/2-sources/CLAUDE|6-politics/2-sources]]. Each was a vault per channel, which the glossary no longer calls a Source.

| Vault | Its Source now |
|---|---|
| `Paduteam/` (Graphiked) | [[6-politics/2-sources/1-paduteam/CLAUDE\|PaduTeam]] |
| `Boniface/` | [[6-politics/2-sources/2-boniface/CLAUDE\|Boniface]] |

**Frozen.** Nothing here gets edited, renamed or added to: no ingestion, no fix, no tidying. A Digest may read a vault's note on the same video as extra input, and that is all. The Digests are rebuilt from the Raws, which carry the timestamps these notes lack.

What each vault had beside its notes moved to its Source in WIK-1: `source.yaml`, the editorial `CLAUDE.md`, and PaduTeam's `Skills/`, now that Source's `.skills/`. A vault's own paths (`BUILD.md`, `Sources/`, `Videos/`, its `_CHRONOLOGIQUE.md` tracker) are still relative to the vault folder, and `legacy.vault` in each `source.yaml` points back here.

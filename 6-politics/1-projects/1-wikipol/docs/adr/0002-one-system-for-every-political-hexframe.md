# Every political hexframe lives in my System

Sources, my World view and the hexframes to come (geopolitics, French parties) all hang under Politics in my own System, Sources beside the projects that build it. In v1 the repo is the truth, and it is one vault: kept there, these hexframes cite each other's Digests with wikilinks that resolve, and a sync of the vault to the app later lands them in one System. A Wikipol Organization holding the Sources would be easier to share, and pins (a Reference to a readable Tile of another System, HEX-133) would let my World view cite it; it is left for when the app is synced, since moving the Sources out then changes their links from wikilinks to pins.
